## Unicode and CLDR data (symbols)

Symbol code points, names and keywords are generated at build time from the Unicode Character Database and the Unicode CLDR annotations. They are licensed under the Unicode License v3 (https://www.unicode.org/license.txt). The generated files are not stored in the repository.

## Text faces

The faces in tools/data are original compositions written for this project.

## Word lists

The Arabic and English word lists and the next-word pairs in core/src/main/res/raw (words_ar, words_en, pairs_ar, pairs_en) are original compilations written for this project, including common Egyptian, Levantine, Gulf and Maghrebi words. No third-party dictionary is bundled. Open dictionaries such as Wiktionary frequency lists are under share-alike terms and need a licence review before they are added.
